Job Description

As mentioned above, I am technically the coop for the CIO (Chief Information Officer), but I have been assigned to a project team in Enterprise Services for this term. The main project I have worked on is Sharepoint Migration, specifically with powerapp forms. When something needs to be done / reported internally at the TTC, a form needs to be filled out. This can range from damage to a train, a dispute with a passenger, uniform expenses, etc. The forms that they need to fill in are currently on the Sharepoint 2013 ecosystem. During last term, my job mainly revolved around copying the design of the old infopath forms to the new powerapps forms, making them all line up well, and making them as close to the previous forms as possible. This term, I focused mainly on automation and workflow. Each form had one or more unique workflows, so we had to make the main system capable of handling and configuration of many different workflow types and workflow steps. I learned a lot about power automate throughout the term while working on workflows.
