Employer

This work term I am working at the TTC in the IT Services Department. The main goal of the department is to build and maintain the tools used for daily operation at the TTC. I am technically the CIO's Co-op student, but for this coop term, I was assigned to a specific project under Enterprise Services management. Enterprise services generally deals with the operations of the business, creating new tools, migrating old tools, and supporting other teams with certain technical tasks. During this coop term, I have mainly been on one project, which I will detail better in the "Job Description" portion, but is a project to migrate old sharepoint to new sharepoint. Our whole company relies on SP for our daily operation, including the Intranet, so it is important to make it look good and keep it in working, maintainable order.

Job Description

As mentioned above, I am technically the coop for the CIO (Chief Information Officer), but I have been assigned to a project team in Enterprise Services for this term. The main project I have worked on is Sharepoint Migration, specifically with powerapp forms. When something needs to be done / reported internally at the TTC, a form needs to be filled out. This can range from damage to a train, a dispute with a passenger, uniform expenses, etc. The forms that they need to fill in are currently on the Sharepoint 2013 ecosystem. Unfortunately, Microsoft doesn't have a convenient way to migrate these forms to new styles / functionality, so it has been my job to migrate these forms by understanding all the different parts of the original form, and remaking it in powerapps. I have created tools to do it slightly automatically, but it is mainly manual design and configuration. I have also been responsible for making workflows, and documentation for all of the forms, as we work on getting closer to UAT and release.
